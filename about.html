Who we are

Our story, mission, and the community we've built over decades.

Founded in 1999, NIS has been a cornerstone of quality education in the Springfield community. We believe every child deserves a nurturing, inclusive, and intellectually stimulating environment.

Our dedicated faculty work hand-in-hand with families to ensure students not only excel academically but also grow as responsible, compassionate individuals ready to take on the world.
